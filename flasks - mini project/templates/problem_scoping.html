What
What is the problem?

Clearly define the specific problem or challenge that needs to be solved. This involves understanding:
  • The current pain points in the process
  • What outcomes are expected
  • What constraints exist
  • What success looks like
A well-defined problem is half the solution.
💡
Why
Why are we solving this?

Understand the underlying motivation and business value:
  • What business value will be created
  • What costs will be reduced
  • What competitive advantage will be gained
  • What customer needs will be addressed
This helps align the project with strategic goals.
👥
Who
Who is involved?

Identify all stakeholders and users:
  • End users who will interact with the solution
  • Business owners and decision makers
  • Technical teams who will build and maintain
  • Customers and partners affected by the solution
Understanding users is crucial for success.
🌍
Where
Where will it be used?

Define the context and deployment environment:
  • Physical or digital locations of deployment
  • Existing systems that need integration
  • Data sources and availability
  • Regulatory and compliance requirements
Context determines technical requirements.
